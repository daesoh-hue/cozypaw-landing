Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CozyPaw.

How long does it take to see results?
Most customers report visible improvement within 30 days. Some notice changes in as little as 14 days. Results vary by individual dog and their specific health needs.
What's your guarantee?
We offer a 60-day money-back guarantee. If your dog doesn't improve, simply return your order for a full refund—no questions asked.
Are your products vet-approved?
Yes! All CozyPaw supplements are formulated with veterinary guidance and recommended by vets across the country. We partner with leading veterinary nutritionists.
Can I use multiple products together?
Absolutely! Many customers use multiple supplements to address different aspects of their dog's health. Consult with your vet if you have specific concerns.
