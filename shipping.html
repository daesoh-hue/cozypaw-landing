Shipping & Returns

Shipping Information

We're committed to getting your CozyPaw products to you quickly and safely.

Shipping Method Delivery Time Cost
Standard Shipping 5-7 business days FREE on orders over $50
Express Shipping 2-3 business days $9.99

📦 Free shipping on all orders over $50! We want to make it easy for you to give your senior dog the best care.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays). You'll receive a confirmation email with your order details immediately after purchase.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and carrier information. You can use this to track your package in real-time. Tracking is available for all shipping methods.

International Shipping

We currently ship to Canada and selected international locations. Contact us for international shipping rates and estimated delivery times at shipping@cozypaw.com.

60-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We're confident you and your dog will love CozyPaw. If for any reason you're not satisfied with your purchase, we offer a hassle-free 60-day money-back guarantee.

How it works:
  1. Purchase any CozyPaw product
  2. Use it for up to 60 days
  3. If unsatisfied, contact us within 60 days of delivery
  4. Receive a full refund, no questions asked

Returns & Refunds Process

  1. Contact Us: Email support@cozypaw.com with your order number and reason for return within 60 days of delivery.
  2. Receive Return Authorization: We'll send you a prepaid return shipping label and return instructions.
  3. Ship It Back: Pack your product securely and drop it off at any shipping location. For unopened/unused products, we'll cover return shipping.
  4. Process Refund: Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days.
  5. Refund Issued: The refund will be credited to your original payment method.

Return Eligibility

Eligible for return:

  • Within 60 days of delivery
  • Product in original packaging (unopened supplements)
  • Accessories in like-new condition
  • All original documentation included

Non-returnable items:

  • Products opened/used and past 60-day window
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse
  • Digital products or downloadable content

Damaged or Defective Items

If your product arrives damaged or defective, contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We'll replace the item or issue a full refund right away—no questions asked.

Email: support@cozypaw.com

Partial Refunds

If you've used a significant portion of the product but are within the 60-day window, we may offer a partial refund. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

Shipping Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address, contact us immediately. We can often update the address before your order ships. Once shipped, you can redirect the package through your carrier or contact us for further assistance.

Customs & Import Duties

For international orders, customers are responsible for any applicable customs duties, import taxes, or fees. CozyPaw is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance.
